There were unused colors in NFS 3 on PlayStation, but there were three, now there is at least 20 unused, cool vehicle colors you can use for your cars, unearthed by me! To use a color for the player car or opponent car, find the corresponding addresses and apply the Color ID to the last 2 digits, load the race, and the vehicle will use that color! You can also use colors from different selectable cars into your car, such as the Maroon color from El Nino with the Ferrari 550 Maranello, and vice versa. Below is the gameshark codes and the list of Extra unused colors you can use. Have fun with this!
